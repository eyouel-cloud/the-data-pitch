Privacy Policy

Effective: July 25, 2026

The Data Pitch operates original football-analysis publishing tools and social accounts. We process only information needed to connect an authorized account and publish or measure approved content.

Information processed

This can include platform account identifiers, display names, granted permissions, encrypted authorization tokens, post metadata, publishing status, and platform analytics. Tokens are stored in a private encrypted environment and are never placed in this public repository.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate authorized accounts, publish requested content, maintain publishing records, and review performance. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain connection and publishing records only as needed for operations, security, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can revoke an app connection in the relevant platform settings. For privacy questions or deletion requests, open an issue at the Data Pitch GitHub repository.
